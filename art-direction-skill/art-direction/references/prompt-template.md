# Prompt template (6 parts)

| # | Part | Example |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | Genre & mood | Editorial still-life photograph, quiet-luxury campaign style. |
| 2 | Product (colour + material) | A champagne-gold instant Polaroid-style camera |
| 3 | Unexpected place + "as if it were one of them" | sits inside a vintage Parisian pâtisserie glass display case, among croissants and macarons, as if it were one of the pastries. |
| 4 | Story detail | A small handwritten cream card price tag in front of it. |
| 5 | Text | On the print in elegant black serif: "YASSI", below in smaller italic serif "coming soon". |
| 6 | Look | (signature LOOK line from style-guide.md, unchanged) |

## Fill-in version

```
Editorial still-life photograph, quiet-luxury campaign style. [PRODUCT, colour + material]
sits [UNEXPECTED PLACE], placed among [SURROUNDING OBJECTS], as if it were one of the
[THOSE OBJECTS]. [ONE SMALL STORY DETAIL]. [TEXT ELEMENT] in elegant black serif
typography: "[BRAND]" and below it in smaller italic serif "[MESSAGE]". Soft diffused
daylight, subtle direct flash highlights, warm cream and beige palette, muted pastel
accents, shallow depth of field, slight 35mm film grain, shot on medium format, eye-level
frontal composition, photorealistic, magazine quality.
```

## Motion prompt (one-motion rule)

```
[Describe the still image briefly]. Only [ONE ELEMENT] moves: [SMALL MOVEMENT, a few
centimetres, slow]. Everything else perfectly still. Static locked-off camera, no zoom,
no pan. 5 seconds.
```
