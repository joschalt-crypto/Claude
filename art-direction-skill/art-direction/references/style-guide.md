# Style guide — quiet-luxury editorial

Reference: @aluncreative (Amit Lunenfeld) — campaign visuals for brands made with AI.

## Look
- **Palette:** cream, off-white, warm beige, muted earth tones; muted pastels;
  ONE accent colour max per image.
- **Light:** soft diffused daylight or studio light; subtle direct-flash highlights;
  realistic shadows.
- **Texture:** slight 35mm film grain; tactile materials — paper, linen, frosted/milky glass,
  matte plastic, brushed steel.
- **Camera:** medium format; shallow depth of field; eye-level frontal or clean top-down;
  macro inserts for details (hands, labels, prints).
- **Props/era:** 80s–90s analog objects (corded phones, fax machines, toasters, photo booths,
  filing cabinets, cassette players), vintage shops and hotels.
- **People (if any):** editorial model casting, minimalist tailored wardrobe (silks,
  tailored neutrals), slicked-back hair, statement jewellery.
- **Typography:** elegant black serif; secondary line in smaller italic serif.

## Signature LOOK line (paste unchanged at the end of every prompt)
Soft diffused daylight, subtle direct flash highlights, warm cream and beige palette, muted
pastel accents, shallow depth of field, slight 35mm film grain, shot on medium format,
eye-level frontal composition, photorealistic, magazine quality.

## Never
- Plain white-background packshots
- Red "SALE" banners, loud gradients, neon
- Busy compositions with many competing objects
- Big camera moves in video
