# Studio Yacy: brand rules

Use these rules for every Studio Yacy post. They override the general style guide where they differ.
Source: Studio Yacy brand book (Basel, 2026).

## Who
A small social media studio in Basel, Switzerland, founded in 2026 by Fe. Instagram: @studio.yacy.
Clients are small shops, cafés, studios and makers: owners, not marketers.
Languages: German (Swiss Standard German, addressing the reader as *du*) and English.

## Services (use these exact six)
1. **Your look:** a visual identity for the feed (colours, type, moodboard, Canva templates)
2. **Content:** photos and short videos made in the shop, captions in German and English
3. **A plan:** a monthly content plan built around the season, the stock and Basel's calendar
4. **Being found:** the Google profile, kept tidy
5. **Ads, planned:** simple local ads written out step by step; the client runs them in their own account
6. **A monthly report:** one page covering what happened, why, and what comes next

How we work: Briefing → Your look → Templates → A plan. Clients keep their account, photos and everything we make.

## Beliefs (the creative compass)
Good brands don't shout. Post less, mean more. Consistency beats going viral. Feeds with a point.
Calm, editorial feeds with real photos, plus a bit of colour, humour and play.
No hype, no fake urgency, no bought followers, no promises we can't keep.

## Voice
- Warm, clear and direct. Short sentences. Questions over claims.
- **Headlines end with a full stop.**
- Emojis rarely, and never in headlines.
- Do write: "Which three words describe your shop?" · "Consistency beats going viral." · "Say hello. / Schreib uns."
- Never write: "We know exactly what your brand needs." · "Go viral overnight!" · "Book now! Only 3 spots left!"

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | #F1EFEA | light backgrounds, text on dark |
| Espresso | #3A2D2D | dark backgrounds, logo on light |
| Dusty blue | #9BAABE | the only accent: the logo dot, highlights. Never small text on cream. |
| Ink | #111111 | text on light |
| Stone | #6E6A64 | secondary text on light |
| Light stone | #B9B4AC | secondary text on dark |
| Butter | #EEDB9B | in testing as a playful second accent, not decided yet |

Cream and espresso are the base. In image prompts, say: "cream and warm espresso-brown palette,
one small dusty-blue accent".

## Type
- **Zodiak italic:** headlines, soft words
- **Helvetica Bold caps:** punch words only. **Helvetica Regular:** body, labels, captions.
- Signature mix: "Small *businesses*, THINK *big*."

## Logo (files in `assets/studio-yacy/`)
- `yacy-logo.svg`: espresso signature, for light grounds
- `yacy-logo-cream.svg`: cream signature, for dark grounds or calm photos
- `yacy-profile-picture.svg` / `-sturdy.svg`: the y in a hairline ring, for the profile picture and small sizes
- A handwritten "Yacy" signature in one fine line. Its tail runs into STUDIO and ends in one blue dot.
- **Never let AI draw the logo.** Leave a calm, empty area (a blank card, a clear wall, a plain corner)
  in the generated image, then place the real logo file on top in Canva or similar.
- Don't redraw, stretch or thicken the line.
- Note: the logo files use #89CFF0 for the dot, while the brand book says #9BAABE. Ask Fe which is correct.

## Prompt add-on for Studio Yacy images
Append this after the general LOOK line:
> Cream and warm espresso-brown palette with one small dusty-blue accent, calm editorial
> composition, real everyday materials, a clear empty area for a logo.
