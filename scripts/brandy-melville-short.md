# Brandy Melville — Short (20–24s)

Vertical short, single presenter (Fe) + B-roll. English VO.
Every factual line is sourced at the bottom. Read the "Legal / accuracy" section
before shooting — two claims from the earlier draft were changed because they
were not supported by reporting.

---

## FINAL SCRIPT

**[0–6s] HOOK — Fe, direct to camera, serious**

> "A former Brandy Melville executive says this was the CEO's rule:
> *'If she was Black, if she was fat… he didn't want them in the store.'*"

*On-screen text:* `EX-EXEC, IN A 2020 LAWSUIT` + the quote, in quotation marks.

**[6–13s] BUILD — VO over B-roll (storefront, racks, all-white shelves)**

> "One size fits all wasn't a slogan. It was a filter.

**[13–19s] INFO — dry, flat delivery**

> "Then the executive group chat leaked. Three years of it.
> Racist and antisemitic 'jokes' — the CEO put his own face on Hitler."

*On-screen text:* `Business Insider, 2021` (blur/redact any screenshot B-roll)

**[19–24s] KICKER — Fe, shrug**

> "Consequences? He's still the CEO. Stores are still open.
> So — is it still in your closet?"

## LEGAL / ACCURACY — what changed from the draft and why

1. **"people of color" → "Black"**
   The reported allegation is specifically about Black customers. Widening it
   makes the claim easier to attack and harder to source. Narrow = credible.

2. **"To an employee" → "a former executive, in a lawsuit"**
   The quote comes from Luca Rotondo, a former senior VP who sued in 2020.
   Note for fairness: **his case was dismissed.** A separate case brought by the
   former Canadian franchise heads over refusing discriminatory hiring went the
   plaintiffs' way. Don't say "proven in court" about either.

3. **"150 screenshots" — CUT.** No reporting supports that number. What is
   reported: a group chat ("Brandy Melville gags") running roughly 2017–2020,
   about 30 members, with Marsan making at least 24 references to Hitler,
   including a photo with his face on Hitler's body. The Hitler detail is both
   verifiable and a harder punch than a headcount.

4. **Attribution up front, not hedged in the middle.**
   "A former exec says…" costs half a second and is stronger than "allegedly"
   buried mid-sentence — it also keeps the claim defensible.

5. **Marsan and Brandy Melville did not respond to press requests for comment.**
   If you want to be bulletproof, add one card at the end:
   `Marsan and Brandy Melville did not respond to requests for comment.`
   It reads as authority, not as a walk-back.

6. **Never state it as settled fact in the caption either.** Caption should
   carry the same attribution as the VO.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Hook must land inside 2s: cold open on Fe's face, no logo, no intro sting.
- The quote is the hook. Show it as text simultaneously — people watch muted.
- Keep the group-chat beat flat and unemotional; the contrast does the work.
- Kicker is the engagement driver: the question needs to be the last audio,
  with 0.5s of silence on Fe's face before the cut.
- Blur any real screenshots/faces in B-roll. Do not show slurs on screen.
